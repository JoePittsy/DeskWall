# Surface: the desktop wallpaper layout (`layouts/alpine-rice.json`)

Mode: Experience (the photo leads; the interface recedes) with an Operate strip in the right margin.
Audience: one person, at a docked ultrawide, mostly at night, the desktop visible in the gaps between windows.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A dark alpine photograph with one cold accent drawn along the real ridge. It refuses the
"every feature gets a colour" dashboard of the previous layout: one palette, one accent, data in one
column, nothing decorative that is not tied to a value.

OWN-WORLD: Nord-derived. Ground: the photo, graded by phase (night/dusk dominant). Ink `#ECEFF4`
at 80-90 %, dim `#D8DEE9` at 60 %. Accent frost `#88C0D0`; secondary `#81A1C1` for the CPU line;
warn `#EBCB8B`; danger `#BF616A`; tracks `#D8DEE9` at 30 %. One family: Bahnschrift (Light for the
clock, Regular for data, SemiCondensed nowhere). Lines 3 px, glow 8-12 px. No washes, no halos, no
sun-glow discs; weather and heat layers exist but in palette colours at 25-45 % alpha.

STORY: glance at the ridge for volume, the foothills for load, the right column for the machine,
the bottom-left for what is playing. Colour means state: amber warns, red means act.

FIRST VIEWPORT: the ridge line in frost across the full width at y≈330-850; the clock in
Bahnschrift Light 160 centred over the main peak (x≈1763), y 48; right column x 3000-3420: weather,
four 96 px dials with 22 px values, drive bars, battery/peers/reboot lines in 18-20 px; bottom-left
now-playing: 160 px art, 32/22 px title/artist, a 4 px progress bar. Sun 140 px, moon 120 px on
their arcs, no halos. Stars small and white, night only.

FORM: pinned by the owner in the structured question ("Rice: dark, one palette"; "Replace the
clock"). No seed key: a user-pinned direction beats the roll (new-work.md §3).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the
verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
